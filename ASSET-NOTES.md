The other photos came from Unsplash URLs in the uploaded project. Bundled WebP copies are named after those original photo IDs. Photo variants at 160px, 320px, 480px and 800px support thumbnails and smaller screens; original-width copies remain available for larger displays. The transparent hero also has 320px, 480px and 800px variants. These are technical resizes of the existing visuals.

There are 129 WebP files. `src/data/imageManifest.json` records their actual dimensions, and the image component advertises only existing candidates. The hero preload and component share the same candidate list. Light-profile photos omit candidates wider than 800px while preserving the full hero preload list to avoid an extra hero download.
